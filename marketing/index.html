Simple Process

Book in 3 easy steps

No phone calls. No waiting. From discovery to confirmed appointment in under 2 minutes.

1

Discover

Browse hundreds of verified salons and services near you. Filter by distance, price, rating, or category and view real-time availability.

2

Book & Pay

Pick your time slot, choose a stylist, and pay securely with Stripe. Your appointment is locked instantly — zero risk of double booking.

3

Earn Rewards

Every booking earns loyalty points. Climb from Bronze to Platinum tier and redeem points for discounts on future appointments.

Platform Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

🗺️

Location-Aware Discovery

Smart geo search surfaces the best salons in your area with live availability — no more calling ahead to check if they're free.

⚡

Real-Time Slot Locking

The moment you confirm, your slot is locked via Redis. No double bookings, no awkward calls — your time is yours.

💬

In-App Messaging

Chat directly with your salon. Share inspiration, ask questions, and receive before-and-after photos — all in one thread.

🏆

Loyalty Rewards Program

Earn 10 points per dollar spent. Four tiers — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum — each with better perks and deeper discounts.

🔔

Smart Reminders

Automated push notifications 24 hours before your appointment. You'll never accidentally miss a booking again.

🔒

Secure Stripe Payments

Industry-standard encrypted payments. Transparent refund policy — 100% back if a vendor cancels, prorated if you do.

📋

Intelligent Waitlist

Fully booked? Join the waitlist. If a spot opens, you're automatically promoted and notified — no manual checking needed.

⭐

Verified Reviews Only

Every rating comes from a verified completed booking. Fake reviews are impossible — so every star you read is earned.
